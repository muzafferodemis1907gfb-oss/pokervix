This notice covers the edition that displays Google AdMob ads but does not use the developer’s own account or online matchmaking server. It must be updated if analytics, Play Games sign-in, or other SDKs are added.

1. Controller and scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the POKERVIX Poker mobile game (package: com.victoriumsoft.pokervix), developed by Victorium Soft. For privacy inquiries, contact victoriumsoft@gmail.com.

2. Game data stored on your device

Game progress, in-game points/virtual chips, scores, and preferences may be stored locally on your device to operate the game and remember your settings. These records do not inherently require you to provide your name, email address, or telephone number.

3. Categories and purposes of data processing

Victorium Soft may keep game progress, scores, and preferences on your device. To deliver ads, measure advertising performance, prevent abuse, and support technical operation, Google AdMob may process device model/operating system information, IP address, inferred approximate location, ad interactions, app diagnostics, and—depending on device permissions and settings—the Android advertising ID. Not all categories are necessarily processed on every device or session. Google’s notices govern the details and retention periods of its advertising data processing.

4. Third parties and sharing

Google AdMob (Google LLC) may be used to deliver advertising. Information handled by the advertising SDK may be processed by Google and, where applicable, its advertising technology partners. Victorium Soft has limited direct control over such processing. Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising technologies.

4A. Ad choices and consent

Where required under the rules applicable in your region, appropriate consent choices must be offered for ad personalization and related data processing. You can also manage advertising ID and personalization settings through your device’s privacy/ad controls. Available options vary by country, device, and software version.

5. Security

Locally stored game data benefits from protections provided by your device and operating system. When data is transmitted over the internet, the relevant service provider’s security practices apply. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Retention and deletion

Locally stored game records and settings may remain until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it. If operating-system backup and restore features are enabled, management of backup copies may depend on the relevant platform’s settings and terms. You can submit privacy-related requests using the email address below.

7. Your choices and rights

You can clear app data and manage supported permissions through your device settings. You may contact us with access, correction, or deletion requests concerning personal data. We will assess requests according to applicable laws and technical capabilities.

8. Children’s privacy

We do not intend to knowingly request names, contact details, or other personal information from children. If you have a concern about a child’s privacy, please contact us. The app’s audience and age rating are separately specified on its Google Play listing.

9. Updates to this notice

We may update this notice if the game’s data practices or services change. Material changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
